HA-FindMy is an independent Home Assistant custom integration and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple Inc.

Parts of the local Bluetooth matching and Home Assistant integration design were adapted from [malmeloo/hass-FindMy](https://github.com/malmeloo/hass-FindMy), which is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0.

Apple Find My protocol functionality is provided by the FindMy.py ecosystem. HA-FindMy currently pins the parawanderer FindMy.py fork used by OpenTagViewer for iCloud Keychain accessory discovery.

HA-FindMy contains modifications and additional functionality developed for this project, including direct iCloud accessory selection, Home Assistant entities, Bermuda integration, diagnostics and accessory management.
